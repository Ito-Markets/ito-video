Blai Product Launch Video (Saas)

A product launch video showcasing Blai, an AI-driven crypto trading app. The video visualizes how users can research, build portfolios, and execute trades through a single chat prompt.


Saas Video Project - Blai Launch Video

About the Brand
Blai is a next-generation crypto trading application that puts AI at the heart of the user experience. With its tagline “Chat to Trade”, the idea is simple yet powerful: rather than navigating complex interfaces, users can converse with the app, research, build a portfolio and execute trades — all in one prompt. Blai positions itself as a smart, intuitive alternative to the outdated crypto UX.


The Problem
The crypto trading landscape is crowded, confusing and intimidating — especially for everyday investors. Interfaces are often clunky, data-dense and built for experienced traders, leaving many novices frustrated or locked out. Blai recognised that barrier and sought to transform the experience by making AI the interface.


The Solution
Blai solves the problem by offering:
An AI-driven assistant that monitors markets, suggests tailored trades, and adapts to the user’s style. blaiapp.io
A seamless workflow: research, portfolio building, and execution all within a single app prompt.
Clean, modern design language that simplifies complex data into clear, actionable visuals.




Creative Direction
For the launch video I crafted for the brand, the creative direction centered on clarity, motion and futuristic trust. The goal was to visually communicate “sophisticated AI made simple”. Key themes and tools included:

- Interface animations to show the chat-to-trade workflow, portfolio dashboards, and AI agents at work.
Clean graphic elements designed in Figma and Illustrator, then animated in After Effects and assembled in Premiere Pro.
- A color palette and typography that reflect Blai’s tech-forward, polished brand identity.
- Dynamic motion graphics under ten seconds (per the brief) to capture attention and convey key benefits succinctly.
- Visual pacing and transitions that reinforce trust, while maintaining a smooth user-centric feel.


Impact
The video launched alongside Blai’s Google Play availability and social post captioned:
“The old crypto UX is obsolete. Starting today, AI is the interface. Only on blai.”
By leveraging sharp motion design and strong storytelling, the piece helped to:

- Elevate Blai’s brand positioning — from one of many crypto apps to the first true “Chat to Trade” solution.
- Simplify and democratize the value proposition, making it accessible to both novice and seasoned traders.
- Provide a high-impact piece of content for social and app-store campaigns, driving awareness and downloads.




Tools Used
– Figma (UI/UX layouts and storyboards)
– Adobe Illustrator (iconography and vector assets)
– Adobe After Effects (animation & motion graphics)
– Adobe Premiere Pro (final edit, sound & export)
